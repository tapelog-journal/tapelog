FAQ

What is the best trading journal?

It depends on your broker, assets and budget. Web platforms suit traders who want sync and replay; spreadsheets suit those who want a one-time cost and private data.

Is there a good free trading journal?

Yes: free plans from some web journals, or free spreadsheet templates such as TapeLog Lite.

Is a spreadsheet good enough for a trading journal?

For core stats like win rate, profit factor, expectancy and drawdown, yes. You give up broker sync and trade replay.
