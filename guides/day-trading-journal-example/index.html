FAQ

What should a day trading journal include?

Date, symbol, side, size, entry, stop, exit, fees, P&L, R-multiple, setup, and a mistake tag or note, plus a pre-market plan and a post-session review.

How often should I review my journal?

Briefly after each session and in depth weekly, looking at stats by setup, time and mistake.

Should I journal losing trades?

Yes. Losing trades and rule breaks are where most of the improvement comes from.
