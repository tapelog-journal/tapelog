Free trading journal template for Google Sheets

Google Sheets is a good free home for a trading journal: it's in your browser, syncs across devices and has no monthly fee. Here's how to build one, or skip ahead and copy a ready-made template.

Formulas (paste into Google Sheets)

Assume net P&L in column J (rows 2–500).

Net P&L per trade:  =IF(C2="Long",(F2-E2)*D2,(E2-F2)*D2)-G2
Win rate:           =COUNTIF(J2:J500,">0")/COUNT(J2:J500)
Profit factor:      =SUMIF(J2:J500,">0")/ABS(SUMIF(J2:J500,"<0"))
Average win:        =AVERAGEIF(J2:J500,">0")
Average loss:       =AVERAGEIF(J2:J500,"<0")
Expectancy:         =AVERAGE(J2:J500)
R-multiple:         =J2/(ABS(E2-H2)*D2)
