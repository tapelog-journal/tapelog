FAQ

What is the TapeLog weekly habit report?

A once-a-week summary of your own trading behavior. It compares this week with your prior weeks and lists which habits showed up more often and which faded, with counts: sizing up after a loss, holding losing trades longer than winners, overtrading bursts, and a time-of-day slump.

Does the habit report tell me what to trade?

No. It only counts things you already did. It never recommends buying, selling or holding any security and gives no picks, price targets, signals or predictions. It is educational and record-keeping software, not investment advice.

How do I get my own report?

Run the free TapeLog AI Coach (an open-source MCP server) in Claude, Cursor or another MCP client, import your DAS Trader or Robinhood CSVs, and ask for your weekly habit report. Subscribe to the newsletter for the weekly reminder and new report features.
