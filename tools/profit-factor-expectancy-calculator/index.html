FAQ

What is profit factor?

Profit factor = gross profit ÷ absolute gross loss. Above 1.0 means winners earned more than losers lost; 1.5 or higher is often considered solid, but sample size matters.

What is trading expectancy?

Expectancy = (win rate × average win) − (loss rate × average loss). It's the average amount you make or lose per trade.

Can I be profitable with a low win rate?

Yes, if your average win is large enough relative to your average loss. A 35% win rate with a 2.5 payoff ratio has positive expectancy.
