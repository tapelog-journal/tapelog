Free trading calculators

Free, private, in-browser calculators for day traders. No sign-up.

  • Position size calculator: Free position size calculator: enter account size, risk %, entry and stop to get the share count, dollar risk and position value. Works for longs and shorts.
  • Risk/reward & R-multiple calculator: Free risk/reward and R-multiple calculator: enter entry, stop, target and exit to get R:R ratio, R-multiple of the result, and breakeven win rate.
  • Profit factor & expectancy calculator: Free profit factor and expectancy calculator: enter win rate, average win and average loss (or gross profit/loss) to get profit factor, expectancy per trade and payoff ratio.
  • Breakeven win rate calculator: Free breakeven win rate calculator: see the minimum win rate you need to break even for your average win, average loss and per-trade fees.
  • Drawdown recovery calculator: Free drawdown recovery calculator: see the percentage gain needed to recover from a drawdown, and how many losing trades in a row cause it at your risk per trade.
  • PDT day trade calculator: Free PDT calculator: enter your day trades in the last 5 business days and account equity to see how many day trades you have left, with FINRA's 2026 PDT rule change.

FAQ

Are these calculators free?

Yes. They run in your browser and send no data anywhere.

Which calculator should I start with?

Start with the position size calculator to set risk, then use the profit factor & expectancy calculator on your journal stats.
