Robinhood trading journal spreadsheet

Robinhood shows your P&L, but not your win rate, profit factor, expectancy, or which setups and hours make you money. TapeLog reads Robinhood's own export.

Steps

  1. In Robinhood go to Account → Reports and statements → Account activity report and download the CSV.
  2. Paste it into TapeLog's Import tab and choose the Robinhood preset.
  3. Stock and ETF Buy/Sell rows become trades. Dividends, transfers, options and crypto rows are skipped automatically.
  4. Check the Dashboard and the PDT counter.

No login or API access is involved. TapeLog never connects to your Robinhood account.
