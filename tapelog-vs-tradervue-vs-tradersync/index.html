TapeLog vs Tradervue vs TraderSync

An honest comparison. Tradervue and TraderSync are full web platforms with broker sync, charts and far more reports. TapeLog is a spreadsheet you own. It's cheaper and private, but it does less.

Which to choose

  • TapeLog if you trade stocks on DAS Trader Pro or Robinhood, want the core stats (win rate, R, profit factor, expectancy, drawdown, breakdowns), and don't want a subscription.
  • Tradervue if you want a web journal with charts on every trade and a free tier for light trading.
  • TraderSync if you want broad broker sync, replay, mobile and AI features and will use them enough to justify the monthly cost.
