TapeLog Trading Journal: a DAS Trader Pro & Robinhood trading journal spreadsheet

TapeLog is a day-trading journal for Excel and Google Sheets (plus a Notion version). Paste a DAS Trader Pro or Robinhood CSV export and it rebuilds your round-trip trades and calculates your stats automatically. It costs $24 once, with no subscription, and a free Lite version.

What it calculates

  • Net P&L, win rate, average win/loss, payoff ratio
  • Profit factor, expectancy ($ and R), average R, total R
  • Max drawdown ($ and %), win/loss streaks, green/red days, equity curve
  • P&L, win % and profit factor by setup, hour of day, weekday and ticker
  • PDT day-trade counter (rolling 5 business days, holiday-aware, on/off switch)
  • Pre-market checklist and risk-based position sizer
